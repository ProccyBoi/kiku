# Kiku / Mame P0 mechanical design

**Engineering development release. Not approved for production tooling.**

This version is generated by `scripts/housing.py` and `parameters.json`. Its outputs are isolated in `exports/P0/`. Other `kiku_*_v1` files in the parent export folder belong to a separate design iteration and are not interchangeable with these parts.

## Hardware basis and coordinate system

The current `Walkman - Blobject.kicad_pcb` is the dimensional authority. `reference/board_datums.json` records its SHA-256, every footprint position, mounting pad/drill, model transform, and board outline. The PCB file is not modified. The exact board outline, including the jack notch and asymmetric bottom-left corner, is retained in the reference STEP.

Mechanical X = KiCad X - 72.11; mechanical Y = 157.90 - KiCad Y; Z = 0 at the nominal board back, positive toward the controls. The nominal board is 60 x 105 x 1.6 mm. The KiCad bare-body STEP omits some conductor thickness; compare its reported Z extent with the nominal 1.6 mm stack before using imported surfaces as mounting datums.

Its specified PEC11L-4120F-S0020 drawing instead puts the nominal shaft tip at Z = 26.1 mm. P0 uses the specified part's dimensions and retains the discrepancy as an incoming inspection item.

## Three concepts and selection

See `concepts/three_concepts.png`, individual front/rear/profile/perspective images, and the three concept STEP files. These are exterior studies sharing the board, display, and control datums; they do not represent three independently finished mechanisms.

| Concept | Assembly approach | Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mame: soft pebble** | Two shells, internal lens carrier, rear screws | Balanced width around a fixed rectangular PCB; broad rear grip; gentle lower-left swell gives identity with screen off; suitable room for isolated cell and speaker | Fuller than the board; long encoder creates local depth; organic surface requires careful finishing |
| **Nagare: offset swell** | Same two-shell stack, larger asymmetric right-hand grip | Most expressive silhouette; thumb-side volume is comfortable for a right-handed grip | Extra width with no hardware benefit; less neutral in the left hand; greater pocket bulk |
| **Mini: restrained capsule** | Two shells with smaller perimeter allowance | Smallest outline; most familiar MiniDisc-era proportions; least plastic | Less room around side bosses and lead dress; flatter identity; tight margins make tolerances harder |

Engineering judgement scores, 1 = weak and 5 = strong; equal weighting:

| Concept | Identity | Ergonomics | Manufacture | Assembly | Robustness | Fixed hardware | Total / 30 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mame | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | **25** |
| Nagare | 5 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 23 |
| Mini | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 21 |

Mame is selected. Scores describe design judgement, not user testing. The front is warm opaque ivory, rear satin muted green, primary key orange, other controls tonal green/ivory, and the window smoked with an opaque reverse mask. The lower front has room for a small printed Kiku wordmark; the rear upper flat has a 26 x 12 mm reserved product-information area. No false vents or decorative grooves are used.

## Stack and construction

**Approximate overall envelope: 77.1 mm wide x 120.9 mm tall x 46.8 mm deep including the knob**, from exported mesh extrema at 0.08 mm tessellation tolerance. The main shell extends from Z = -18.0 to +15.2 mm: **33.2 mm nominal body depth**. Raised keys and encoder add local depth; the knob top is Z = 28.8, so the total nominal front-to-back envelope is **46.8 mm**. This is a material ergonomics limitation of the specified 20 mm encoder shaft and the 10 mm thick battery. The interface review supplies the exported mesh extrema for approximate manufacturing envelope dimensions; the BREP inventory also records bounding boxes, which can overbound curved spline surfaces. Do not substitute a shorter shaft or thinner battery without an explicit hardware decision.

Housing skins use 1.8 mm nominal walls. Plan offsets follow sampled inward curve normals; the front/rear edge rolls approximate 3.5/4.0 mm quarter-rounds with 15-degree CAD segments. This is an explicit prototype construction, not a Class-A mould surface. Surface continuity, local wall mapping and minimum neck sections must be rebuilt/verified for tooling. Internal bridge ribs are 1.4 mm wide and locally 1.0 mm deep. Boss roots require physical torque/drop testing.

### Display and lens

The user permits a floating display above the PCB. Its body is 51.8 x 36.2 x 2.05 mm, with a 40.8 x 30.6 mm active area, from the [HS manufacturer drawing](https://atta.szlcsc.com/upload/public/pdf/source/20251016/BAE15D01F6ECB51EA8350D6C5C8F6372.pdf), sheet 10. The KiCad placement rectangle is 0.01 mm shorter in Y; P0 preserves its centre and uses the drawing's actual body size.

P0 orients the tail toward the PCB's right-side FPC connector. The active area is offset 2.66 mm left of the module centre in this orientation; the aperture follows it rather than centring it blindly in the body. The 41.6 x 31.4 mm aperture provides 0.4 mm nominal overscan per edge. The 54 x 37.8 x 1.0 mm lens is recessed 0.2 mm, with 0.3 mm perimeter clearance and a 0.2 mm adhesive seat. Mask the lens reverse face around the optical aperture. The LCD top is at Z = 12.50, with 0.40 mm nominal clearance to the bezel underside. The removable carrier supports the backlight frame through a thin compliant strip; it does not clamp the active glass.

The FPC route is a reserved folding space, not a proven cable model. Confirm the supplied tail orientation, contact side, usable length, bend radius and latch access with the actual panel before fastening the carrier. Do not sharply crease the FPC.

### Controls

Both large caps have captured flanges and a 0.35 mm nominal radial guide allowance at the exposed cap. A small underside contact feature meets the switch actuator. The actual switch operating stroke, force, and tolerance were not supplied. The housing is checked at 0, 0.5 and 1.0 mm downward cap displacement; this tests housing space, not the switch itself. Verify take-up, overtravel stop and return before powered use. Add a compliant return/anti-rattle element only after measuring the force budget.

SW1/QON remains an internal service control in this iteration; no external plunger is supplied. Confirm the intended user power/wake behaviour in firmware before accepting this interface. The encoder uses a 21 mm outside diameter knob, a 6.24 mm D-shaped nominal socket, an integrated housing guide around its skirt, and a radial M2 grub screw in a metal insert. The [Bourns PEC11L drawing](https://www.bourns.com/docs/product-datasheets/pec11l.pdf) specifies 0.5 mm nominal switch travel with +0.4/-0.3 mm tolerance; P0 reserves 1.2 mm between the knob shoulder and raised island before push. The skirt counterbore extends above the full-round shaft section; the D socket starts where the flat is available. Housing clearance is checked at 0, 0.45 and 0.9 mm downward knob displacement. Check the installed shaft length and knob axial setting over the whole 0.2-0.9 mm switch stroke.

### Battery

The user-specified cell envelope is **34 x 50 x 10 mm**, reported capacity 200 mAh. Capacity is not inferred from its physical size. Its nominal location is X = 13..47, Y = 45..95, Z = -15.2..-5.2. Foam supports the pouch; side rails provide 0.5 mm nominal lateral clearance. A removable insulating guard at Z = -3.8 leaves **1.4 mm nominal space above the pouch**, with no intentional compression from shell screws. Supplier dimensional tolerance, protection board, lead exit, and swelling requirements remain unverified. That 1.4 mm is a packaging reserve, not a claim that a damaged/swollen cell is safe to keep using.

The guard and its fasteners remain outside the nominal cell footprint. Radius/finish every pouch-facing edge. Use a pull ribbon under the battery and disconnect it before service; do not permanently bond the cell into the case. Confirm the connector's cable bend and unplugging space. A printed guard is a prototype; a smooth insulating sheet is preferable once its shape is validated.

### Speaker

Selected part: **XHXDZ 20MM-8ohm2W-JFHM, LCSC C50387209**. Its [manufacturer drawing hosted by LCSC](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/8e0aa0a2b86c456182d861902de24ed3.pdf?productCode=C50387209) gives 20 +/- 0.2 mm diameter and 5.1 +/- 0.3 mm total depth. The alternative [C50387215](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C50387215.html) is 30 mm; it is not interchangeable with this retainer.

The selected driver fires toward seven rounded rear slots. A soft rim gasket spaces the diaphragm from the grille. A separate screwed cover and compliant rear perimeter support retain the driver. The pocket is 21.0 mm diameter, giving 0.5 mm nominal radial clearance and 0.4 mm with the maximum listed diameter. A small rear cavity and a sealed wire notch avoid a direct uncontrolled front-to-back air path. The pull-tab outline is not fully dimensioned by the manufacturer; its relief is provisional. Seal the relief with compliant material, then check cone travel, rattles and acoustic leakage. The cavity has not been acoustically tuned or measured; no SPL/bass claim is made.

### Ports and antennas

The USB-C passage is a 13 x 7 mm overmould gauge following the actual mating axis; the headphone passage accepts an 11 mm cylindrical overmould on the real jack axis. These are explicit test gauges, not a guarantee for every cable. Entrance edges require a small deburr/chamfer after printing. Check full seating with a straight plug, a bulky plug, and multiple USB-C cables. The opening supports a contacting cable overmould laterally, but connector solder joints still carry loads until contact; no separate connector strain-relief rating is claimed.

The microSD tunnel and enlarged top finger relief extend back toward the socket so a thumbnail can reach the card. Verify the actual push-push latch, card lock/eject positions and fingertip access with the lens/carrier installed. The insertion corridor intentionally overlaps the reference connector where the card mates; it must clear the plastic housing.

No metallic decoration is used. Keep the lower-right BM83 antenna region free of new metal. [Microchip's module integration guidance](https://onlinedocs.microchip.com/oxy/GUID-414904F5-364E-4377-B959-9226AD29D6A9-en-US-7/GUID-495EA464-310A-41EC-A892-5E46727B10FC.html) calls for at least 15 mm between the PCB trace antenna and external metal for best range. The supplied BM83 STEP places the antenna trace solids at X = 55.34..58.74, Y = 4.78..16.78, Z = 2.2..2.3 mm. A conservative bounding-box audit against added metal gives a minimum separation of 18.32 mm (PCB screw H8), 30.34 mm to the battery envelope and 29.82 mm to the speaker envelope. The interface review records each checked item. This geometric separation is not RF validation. The FM coax guide runs under the PCB on the right. The U.FL transition and final radiating antenna are provisional; the coax alone is not treated as an FM antenna.

## Fasteners and service sequence

Nominal designed interface allowances (not a measured whole-assembly minimum):

| Interface | Allowance | Qualification |
|---|---:|---|
| Large key stem / guide | 0.35 mm per side | Fit coupon; switch return and process texture |
| Encoder skirt / guide | 0.30 mm radial | Rotation and push under side load |
| Lens perimeter / pocket | 0.30 mm per side | Lens cutting tolerance and adhesive thickness |
| LCD face / bezel underside | 0.40 mm | Installed module and carrier tolerance |
| Speaker diameter / pocket | 0.50 mm radial nominal; 0.40 mm at max listed driver diameter | Pocket process tolerance still additional |
| Battery / side rails | 0.50 mm per side | Pouch, protection board and lead tolerances unknown |
| Battery top / insulating guard | 1.40 mm nominal | Verify real cell and supplier expansion guidance |
| FM coax / guide groove | 0.30 mm radial | Provisional 1.4 mm cable diameter |
| M2 screw / nominal clearance hole | 0.10 mm radial | Finish 2.2 mm holes to fit; do not force screws |
| Seam locating tabs / pockets | 0.30 mm per side | Check seam fit after print cleanup |

One screw diameter, M2, is used throughout: four M2 x 18 case screws, four M2 x 6 PCB screws, ten M2 x 4 carrier/guard/speaker screws, and one M2 x 6 grub screw. Nineteen insert envelopes are modelled at 3.2 mm OD x 4.0 mm length; this is a design envelope, not a verified supplier part number. Select a matching insert and qualify its installation bore/process before printing the whole housing. CAD fasteners are plain shank/head/thread-envelope representations, not helical threads. PCB screws are 6 mm long, small carrier/guard/speaker screws 4 mm, rear enclosure screws 18 mm, and the knob uses a 6 mm grub screw. Confirm lengths against the final purchased head geometry, insert specification and blind-hole depth. Do not tighten a screw that bottoms in a blind hole. PCB mounts land on the existing grounded mounting pads; visually inspect surrounding copper before torque testing.

1. Install and inspect threaded inserts in the printed shells and knob.
2. Fit the lens with its removable perimeter adhesive/mask and install the LCD/carrier with compliant frame supports.
3. Place the two large caps in the front guides with the front face supported on a soft assembly nest.
4. Connect/fold the FPC, then place the PCB on the four real mounting datums and install its screws. Verify every button's return.
5. Fit the encoder knob at the checked axial setting and tighten the radial grub screw.
6. Install the rear speaker gasket, driver, rear compliant ring, and screwed cover; dress and seal its leads.
7. Fit the foam and battery in the rear pocket; install its removable guard and dress the FM coax.
8. Connect battery and speaker while the shells are open beside each other. Fold leads into the reserved space, close the shells and tighten the four rear screws in stages.
9. Check power, both keys, encoder rotation/push, USB plug seating, headphones, microSD ejection and speaker buzz before normal use.

The battery and PCB are serviceable by screws. Only the display lens uses adhesive. A practical assembly nest and temporary tape may be useful during a first build; permanent tape must not substitute for missing retention.

## Manufacturing and validation limits

Preferred first builds: SLS PA12 or tough SLA resin for geometry evaluation; carefully oriented PETG/ABS/ASA FDM for fit development. Do not rely on heat-set inserts in brittle standard SLA resin. Use process-compatible press/bonded inserts for resin, with the bore requalified. FDM shell faces need support under curved skins; remove support carefully at lens edges and guides. Print a clearance/insert coupon before a complete enclosure. STL units are mm; parts are exported in assembly coordinates and should be laid onto appropriate print faces in the slicer.

For an FDM first article, orient the rear with its outside back toward the bed and cavity upward; support the initial curved perimeter where the slicer requires it. Orient the front with its cosmetic face toward a supported build surface and its open interior upward; protect the window seat during support removal. Put the knob's closed top toward the bed so its shaft socket opens upward. Print carrier, guard and speaker retainer flat; keep supports out of insert bores. Orient key caps to support the flange and small underside contact feature. These are starting orientations requiring slicer review, not support-free printing claims. Prefer SLS for the first complete cosmetic fit build.

Before injection moulding: replace faceted meridian rolls with smooth drafted surfaces; perform a real wall-thickness/draft/undercut audit; reduce boss sink risk with cored/ribbed roots; add material-specific shrink; split/slide-tool the lateral connector openings and guide details; resolve ejection, gates, cosmetic parting line and texture. P0 is not mould-ready tooling geometry.

The machine-readable `review/clearance_review.json` is the source for the actual checks performed. `review/interface_review_P0.json` additionally records nine discrete control positions with zero shell overlap, six potentially adjacent fastener/reference pairs with zero overlap (other pairs are separated by bounding boxes), the conservative antenna separation audit, and external mesh dimensions. It distinguishes surface screening from exact collision volumes. It must not be represented as complete tolerance validation. Remaining physical gates include real component/model agreement, all cap strokes, cable/plug/card access, FPC/coax dressing, insertion/torque/pull-out, drop strength, battery lead/swelling reserve, acoustic leakage/buzz, RF range, charging heat, and pocket/one-hand ergonomics.

## Verification result

The nominal check set covers both shells, the display carrier and lens, battery guard, speaker retainer, both caps, the encoder knob, PCB/components and separate hardware envelopes. Explicit gauges cover USB-C (13 x 7 mm), headphone overmould (11 mm diameter), microSD insertion/finger access and the provisional FPC folding space. Control end positions and metal/antenna separation are in the supplementary interface review. All eight current prototype STL meshes are closed with consistent winding and one connected body. Results apply to this P0 inventory; they do not establish continuous-motion clearance, local wall thickness, complete containment or a tolerance-certified assembly.

## Editable source and deliverables

`parameters.json` and `scripts/housing.py` are the native editable CadQuery source. This is a feature-generating program, not a FreeCAD/Fusion feature tree. References remain separate named assembly components. STEP and individual prototype STL exports are in `exports/P0/`; concept STEP/views are in `concepts/`; assembled, exploded, interior and section images are in `review/P0_*`. Rebuild with the isolated environment:

The public package excludes transformed component-reference BREPs, the populated
PCB STEP and fit-check assembly STEP because those files serialise third-party
component models. `REFERENCE_GEOMETRY.md` documents how to obtain the exact local
inputs under their original terms before repeating the full strict reference QA.
The checked-in JSON reports retain the reviewed results without redistributing
those model solids.

```powershell
& Mechanical/.venv/Scripts/python.exe Mechanical/scripts/housing.py
& Mechanical/.venv/Scripts/python.exe Mechanical/scripts/verify.py
& Mechanical/.venv/Scripts/python.exe Mechanical/scripts/render_p0.py
```

After a board change, first rerun `extract_board.py` with KiCad's Python and refresh both reference STEP exports. Recheck all dependent geometry before printing.
