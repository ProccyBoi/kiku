# Kiku mechanical enclosure

This folder contains the mechanical source and prototype exports for the Kiku portable audio player. The enclosure is built around the **current KiCad PCB**, not a generic board envelope.

## V8 direction

The first CAD iteration combines the earlier **Pebble clamshell** and **Lens Island** directions:

- 69 x 115 mm rounded handheld body with an 11 mm plan-view corner radius.
- 1.8 mm nominal walls for an SLS/MJF or careful FDM first prototype.
- Flush separate screen lens over the HS20HS072RX LCD.
- **Two paired pebble tactile caps** with hidden split press-fit sockets on SW3/SW4. They are rotated slightly toward each other and sit in shallow organic recesses rather than looking like generic square caps. SW1/QON remains internal.
- **Soft encoder puck** with a hidden split-collet press fit on the PEC11L 6 mm knurled shaft. A recessed M7x0.75 bushing-nut pocket is hidden underneath so the encoder can mechanically support the shell rather than loading only its solder joints.
- Real bottom USB-C and 3.5 mm jack positions from KiCad.
- Softer top microSD access: a narrower card tunnel plus a broad thumb scallop instead of a rectangular cut-out.
- Four M2 PCB standoffs at the actual board holes plus lower-edge support rails.
- Rear-to-front perimeter locating tongue with first-print slip-fit clearance.
- **Two hidden M2 case screws beneath the removable screen lens**, reusing the PCB's existing upper 2.2 mm mounting holes. Rear posts include pockets for small M2 brass inserts, so the finished exterior has no visible enclosure screws.
- **Uniform smooth rear shell** deep enough for the supplied 10 x 34 x 50 mm LiPo. The previous local battery hump has been removed.
- **No internal speaker**. LS1 remains available electrically and a 4.4 mm right-side cable egress is provided for an externally mounted speaker lead.
- **Recessed multimeter-style kickstand** on the rear. The 42 x 56 mm open-frame stand sits nearly flush when closed and uses a 1.9 mm hinge bore intended for ~1.75 mm filament or a similar smooth pin.
- **Left-side SMA bulkhead** for the external FM antenna.
- Reserved 100 mm RF1.13 U.FL-to-SMA cable route from PCB J3, down the right side and then across below the battery, deliberately leaving the BM83 lower-right RF keepout before crossing the case.
- Hinged telescopic antenna stows upward along the left side instead of lying across the rear face, with two open crescent nesting saddles to make the folded whip look intentionally parked.
- Explicit lower-right BM83/RF keepout: no battery foil, speaker magnet, metal boss or antenna-coax bundle should be added there.

V8 deliberately prioritises appearance over absolute minimum thickness. The body remains a uniform **28.7 mm nominal thickness**, absorbing the 10 mm battery without a rear hump and keeping the front speaker-free. Fasteners are hidden beneath the screen lens, the microSD opening is softened into the top edge, and the folded FM whip gains intentional nesting features rather than hanging loose from the SMA hinge.

## Source of truth

`reference/board_datums.json` is generated from `Walkman - Blobject.kicad_pcb` by `scripts/extract_board.py`. Its mechanical coordinate system is:

- X = PCB X - 72.11 mm
- Y = 157.90 mm - PCB Y
- Z = 0 at PCB rear, +1.6 mm at PCB front

Do not hand-edit the KiCad-derived datums. Re-run the extractor after board changes, then rebuild and review the enclosure.

## Build

Project-owned enclosure/part source and exports are redistributable. The strict
hardware fit-check additionally requires the component 3D models referenced by
the KiCad board; those inputs are intentionally not bundled. See
`REFERENCE_GEOMETRY.md` before rerunning a full hardware-reference validation.

From the repository root, after preparing those local references when a full
fit-check is required:

```powershell
python Mechanical/scripts/build_housing.py
```

CadQuery 2.8 or newer is required.

## Exported parts

`exports/` contains project-owned STEP and STL for:

- front shell
- rear shell
- rear kickstand
- screen lens
- left/right button caps
- encoder knob
- housing-only assembly STEP
- individual printable/manufactured parts
- packaging/keepout STEP

The populated PCB fit-check STEP is a local generated artefact because it embeds
third-party component-model geometry. It is intentionally not redistributed.
The public tree retains `reference/pcb_bare.step`, board datums, component
reference metadata, project-owned housing geometry and recorded QA results.

`review/` also contains validation data and front/rear/exploded PNGs generated by:

```powershell
python Mechanical/scripts/render_review.py
```

Third-party datasheet copies, supplier-page captures and component-library 3D
models used during development are intentionally not redistributed by the public
source tree unless their licence is explicitly recorded. The reports retain the
source links and measured requirements needed to reproduce the design review;
project-derived PCB/enclosure geometry remains checked in.

P0/P1 frozen `REF_*.brep` files and populated fit-check assemblies are likewise
local-only. They can be regenerated after obtaining the exact model inputs under
their original terms; the public packaging scripts never copy them into a
redistributable package.

## Parameters that are intentionally not production-frozen

- LiPo pack: V8 uses the supplied **10 x 34 x 50 mm** envelope. The stated capacity is 200 mAh; dimensions are treated as the mechanical source of truth. Final cable exit, protection-PCB position and swelling allowance still need physical verification.
- Speaker: V8 intentionally has **no internal speaker cavity, grille or speaker bulge**. The LS1 PicoBlade output remains on the PCB and exits through the right-side cable passage for an externally mounted speaker.
- Kickstand: V8 uses a recessed open-frame stand with a nominal **62 degree open reference angle**. The first prototype uses hinge friction rather than a moulded detent; tune or drill the 1.9 mm bore for the chosen print process/pin if needed.
- LCD: body and active-area dimensions follow the current HS20HS072RX drawing, but final adhesive/foam stack and FPC bend need a physical fit check.
- FM: the ordered pigtail is an RF1.13 U.FL/IPX-to-SMA bulkhead lead; the 100 mm option is reserved. The ordered telescopic antenna listing shows **105 mm collapsed, 310 mm extended and 9 mm diameter** with a hinge. V8 mounts the SMA on the left side at Y=18 mm, reserves an 88.3 mm internal route plus service slack, and adds open nesting saddles at Y=48 and 84 mm.
- Controls: SW3/SW4 use a measured/modelled 7.2 mm square actuator. V8 caps use a nominal 7.05 mm four-way split socket with a larger lead-in, giving a deliberately small compliant press fit instead of a loose post. The encoder uses a 5.75 mm split-collet bore on the 6.0 mm 18-tooth knurled shaft. PA12 is preferred for these compliant details; FDM users should tune the press fits from the first test print if required.
- Fastening: use two low-profile/button-head M2 screws with **head height <= 1.3 mm** through the upper PCB mounting holes, into the rear post insert pockets. Tighten these before fitting the screen lens; the lens then conceals both screw heads.
- Positive snap retention / cosmetic parting-line refinement / heat-set insert details are deliberately deferred until V1 dimensional fit is verified; V1 includes a locating tongue but is intended to be taped/clamped for the first dimensional fit if required.

## First prototype review

Before treating this as production CAD, verify:

1. PCB drops onto all four posts without stress and the lower cradle does not contact solder joints.
2. USB-C and headphone plugs fully seat through the case openings.
3. microSD can be inserted and removed comfortably through the top thumb scallop with the LCD installed.
4. Both pebble caps press securely onto SW3/SW4, remain removable with deliberate pulling force, and do not preload the switches.
5. Button caps move through the tactile travel without rubbing the front-shell recesses.
6. Encoder split collet grips the 6.0 mm knurled shaft securely; optional M7x0.75 nut/washer clears beneath it and push-to-select still has full travel.
7. Battery does not touch the PCB, mounting screws, coax route or rear-wall features.
8. External speaker lead passes freely through the 4.4 mm right-side egress.
9. Kickstand closes into its recess, rotates freely, and provides useful friction with the selected hinge pin.
10. Hidden M2 screws clamp the shell/PCB stack correctly, their heads stay below the lens underside, and the lens can still be removed for service.
11. SMA bulkhead/nut fits the 6.8 mm left-side hole, U.FL/coax reaches without entering the BM83 keepout, and the hinged antenna rests cleanly in both side saddles without fouling the case.
